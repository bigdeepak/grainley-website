Where tradition meets the now

Grainley Foods makes healthy snacking feel like a celebration of flavour, crunch and Indian roots.

Desi fun, honest food

We grew up on alsi laddoos, til chikki and a bowl of makhana with evening chai. Grainley brings those pantry heroes back, in packs that make you smile.

Our flagship is the mighty makhana, sourced from Mithila, Bihar. Alongside it sit nine everyday seeds, premium quinoa and three natural powders, all raw, unroasted and with no added preservatives.

The boy with the window grin

Every Grainley pack carries our mascot. His open grin is a see-through window, so you always see the real product inside before you buy.
